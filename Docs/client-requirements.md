The Contentstack Sales Engineering team, the Solutions Architect team, our Partners, and other
internal teams are in need of a single source for all of their demonstration needs. Currently, demo
instructions, recordings, and more are scattered across Confluence and Google Drive.
We need to build a site that internal employees and partners can log into in order to find the
materials they need. These include demo setup, instructions, videos, alerts, and feature requests.

Extra information

This project will be built in the same manner that our customers build sites with Contentstack. It’s
an excellent opportunity to learn how companies utilize a CMS to deliver content. While this is an
internal tool, it is still important to create a website with a quality design. We need our users to
adopt the platform so it needs to be easy to navigate and pleasant to use.
We currently use Supabase for our internal logins in our demo tools. It’s fairly straight forward and
we can offer guidance on how to implement it if needed.
We will provide students with a Contentstack environment to work from. We can offer basic training
to get them going and point them to the right documentation. Students will have the opportunity to
interact with professional developers where they can get advice and better learn our processes.
When delivery is ready, we would like the students to present via Zoom to our team of Product
Managers, Sales Engineers, and Solution Architects. This is a great opportunity to be seen and to
learn what questions you can expect on delivery in the real world.
